Disability and Transgender Identity

Introduction: People with cerebral palsy face high levels of discrimination from early childhood into adulthood. This affects their health, wellbeing, and social experiences. When combined with transgender identity, these challenges become even more complex due to overlapping stigma and barriers in healthcare and society.

About the Topic

Cerebral Palsy:

Cerebral Palsy is a group of brain disorders that affect movement and coordination. It develops before birth due to brain damage and varies in severity.

Causes:

  • Brain white matter damage
  • Gene changes
  • Bleeding in the brain
  • Lack of oxygen
  • Maternal infections
  • Stroke
  • Infant infections
  • Traumatic brain injury

Symptoms:

  • Muscle stiffness or weakness
  • Poor coordination
  • Uncontrolled movements
  • Walking difficulties
  • Developmental delays
  • Fine motor skill challenges
