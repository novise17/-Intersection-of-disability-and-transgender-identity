About the Topic

Cerebral Palsy:

Cerebral Palsy is a group of brain disorders/conditions that affect body movement and muscle coordination...

Causes:

  • Brain damage in white matter
  • Gene changes affecting development
  • Bleeding in the brain
  • Lack of oxygen
  • Maternal infections
  • Stroke
  • Infant infections
  • Traumatic brain injury

Symptoms:

  • Muscle stiffness or weakness
  • Poor coordination
  • Uncontrolled movements
  • Walking difficulties
  • Delayed motor skills
  • Fine motor challenges

Transgender Identity & Stigma

Definition:

“Transgender” is a term for a person whose gender identity differs from their sex assigned at birth.

Statistics:

2.14 million adults and 724,000 teens in the U.S. identify as transgender.

Discrimination:

There are 245 anti-trans bills in the U.S., including healthcare restrictions and criminalization of gender expression.

Health:

Some physicians struggle to provide adequate care for transgender individuals and people with disabilities (Mulcahy et al., 2022).
