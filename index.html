Disability and Transgender Identity

Introduction: People with cerebral palsy face high end discrimination, starting from early childhood into adulthood. This affects their health, wellbeing, and social experiences. When combined with other marginalized identities, such as transgender identity, the level of discrimination increases significantly.

About the Topic

Cerebral Palsy:

Cerebral Palsy is a group of brain disorders that affect movement and muscle coordination. It develops before birth due to brain damage and varies in severity.

Causes:

  • Brain white matter damage
  • Gene changes affecting brain development
  • Bleeding in the brain
  • Lack of oxygen
  • Maternal infections
  • Stroke
  • Infant infections
  • Traumatic brain injury

Symptoms:

  • Muscle stiffness or weakness
  • Poor coordination
  • Uncontrolled movements
  • Walking difficulties
  • Delayed motor skills
  • Fine motor challenges
