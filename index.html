About the Topic

Cerebral Palsy:

Cerebral Palsy is a group of brain disorders/conditions that affect body movement and basic muscle coordination. This group of conditions permanently affects body movement and muscle coordination. This condition typically forms before birth, during its time in the womb when it is facing damage to the developing brain. Although it develops before birth symptoms tend to show in early infancy or preschool years. The function and impact of cerebral palsy depends and varies between each person.

Causes:

  • Damage to the brain’s white matter
  • Gene changes that interrupt development of the brain
  • Bleeding in the brain
  • Lack of oxygen
  • Maternal infections that affect the fetus
  • Strokes, which stop the blood flow to the baby’s brain
  • Infant infections that cause swelling and disruption to the developing brain
  • TBI (traumatic brain injury) often caused by falling, physical trauma, or accidents

Symptoms:

  • Spasticity (stiff/tight muscles and unnecessary and exaggerated reflexes)
  • Ataxia (lack of muscle coordination and balance when trying to perform voluntary acts of movement)
  • Dystonia (also known as a tremor, jerky movements and actions you can’t control)
  • Unusual gait (different walking style and trouble walking)
  • Stiff or floppy muscle tone
  • Delays in movement milestones (lifting their head, rolling or sitting, walking, etc)
  • Difficulty with fine motor skills (writing or buttoning a shirt)

Cerebral Palsy Stigma and Discrimination

Cerebral Palsy Is Contagious

A stigma/stereotype of cerebral palsy is that it can be contagious. This is false and even though there is data and real logical evidence to prove this, there is still a stigma stating that cerebral palsy can be passed along if you come close to someone who has it. Cerebral palsy is caused by brain damage during fetal development, so this can NOT be true.

People With CP Are Intellectually Disabled

The stigma that people with CP are not capable of learning or intelligent enough is highly active and hurtful. This can lead to anxiety, depression, and low self-worth.

People with Cerebral Palsy Have a Short Life Span

Life expectancy varies, but many people with CP live long lives with proper healthcare access.

People with Cerebral Palsy Always Are in Wheelchairs

There are different types of cerebral palsy and not all individuals use wheelchairs.

A 2011 study shows that restrictions in the socio-contextual environment influence social exclusion, leading to bullying and discrimination against children with disabilities.

Restrictions in the socio-contextual environment influenced the social exclusion that children experienced. Youth encountered social exclusion from both teachers and peers. Teachers' attitudes toward children with disabilities often influenced peer exclusion. Bullies engaged in both implicit and explicit forms of exclusion, often leading to verbal and physical bullying.

Transgender Stigma in the United States

Transgender stigma and discrimination in the United States is unfortunately extremely common.

Discrimination in the Workplace

Many transgender individuals face job discrimination. “Forty-seven percent (47%) reported adverse job outcomes…” (Grant et al., 2011).

Discrimination in Educational Settings

Transgender individuals also experience stigma in schools due to gender non-conformity and social expectations.

“Those who expressed a transgender identity or gender non-conformity in grades K-12 reported harassment (78%), physical assault (35%), and sexual violence (12%); 15% left school due to harassment” (Grant et al., 2011).

These experiences negatively affect mental health, education, and self-esteem.
